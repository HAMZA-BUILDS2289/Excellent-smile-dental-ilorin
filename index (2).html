Who we are

A dental clinic that puts patients first

Excellent Smile Dental Services is located on Surulere Road, opposite Baptist Church, Ilorin, Kwara. We provide professional dental services with a simple belief: your oral health matters.

Dental care should not feel frightening or confusing. At our clinic, we take time to listen, to explain what we find in plain language, and to agree a plan with you. Your comfort and understanding are as important to us as the treatment itself.

Our services cover the everyday needs of healthy smiles, from check-ups, scaling and polishing, and fillings, to whitening, braces, crowns, bridges and tooth replacement. Whatever brings you to us, our goal is the same: to help you leave with a healthier mouth and a smile you feel good about.

The patient experience

What it feels like to visit us

From the moment you contact us to the moment you leave, we want you to feel cared for. You can book online, by phone or on WhatsApp. When you arrive, your dentist will talk through your concerns, examine your mouth and explain what is needed.

One of our patients described their visit as “delightful” and said they were “treated with kindness and a less painful process”. That is the experience we aim to give every patient.

  • Clear, jargon-free explanations
  • Options discussed before any treatment starts
  • Gentle, considerate care
  • Simple aftercare advice you can follow at home
