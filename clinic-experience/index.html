Inside the clinic

Care in a professional setting

Your dentist wears protective gloves and masks, uses clean dental instruments and works carefully, so you can relax in the chair. We explain what we are doing as we go, and we stop if you need a moment.

Our patients tell us they appreciate the kindness. One wrote that despite worries about how difficult dental treatment can be, they were “treated with kindness and a less painful process”.

  • A clean, professional working environment
  • Gloves, masks and clean instruments
  • Explanations at every step
  • Time to ask questions before and after treatment
